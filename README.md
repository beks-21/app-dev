# The Remarried Empress: A Royal Tale of Resilience

---

## Overview & Background

*The Remarried Empress* (`재혼 황후`) is a South Korean web novel written by **Alphatart**, which was later adapted into a popular webtoon illustrated by **Sumpul**. 

> "I accept the divorce. And I request an approval to remarry." — Empress Navier Trovi

---

## Key Characters

| Character | Original Role | Key Trait / Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Navier Trovi** | Empress of the Eastern Empire | Intelligent, dignified, and deeply committed to her responsibilities as empress. |
| **Sovieshu** | Emperor of the Eastern Empire | The emperor whose poor judgment and infidelity ignite the central conflict. |
| **Prince Heinrey** | Prince / King of the Western Kingdom | A charismatic, cunning ruler who can transform into a bird and wins Navier's heart. |
| **Rashta** | Runaway Slave / Mistress | A former slave brought to court who becomes Empress Sovieshu's concubine. |

---

## Core Plot Highlights

1. **The Royal Betrayal**
   * Emperor Sovieshu rescues a runaway slave, Rashta, and brings her to the palace.
   * Sovieshu elevates Rashta to imperial concubine, undermining Navier's status and duty.
2. **The Bold Counter-Move**
   * Refusing to accept her unfair treatment, Navier agrees to Sovieshu's demand for divorce on one condition: **permission to remarry**.
   * She stuns the imperial court by announcing her instant marriage to King Heinrey of the Western Kingdom.
3. **Reclaiming Destiny**
   * Navier transitions from an underestimated, discarded empress to a powerful monarch ruling alongside Heinrey in the Western Empire.
